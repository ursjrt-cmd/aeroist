Frequently asked questions Straight answers

What is JRT Mart?

JRT Mart is an online shopping storefront. Browse the available categories, view published products and place orders through the website.

What can I shop at JRT Mart?

The category menu includes apparel, uniforms, bags, home and textiles, kitchen and dining, appliances, stationery, accessories and gifting. The live catalogue shows products that have been added and published by the store.

How do I find a product?

Use the search field or browse the mega-menu and category links. Open a product page to review its description, images, price and availability.

How do I place an order?

Open a published product, add it to your cart, review your items and continue to checkout. Enter accurate delivery details and follow the payment instructions displayed at checkout.

Which payment methods are available?

Online payment is provided through Razorpay when the store administrator has configured and enabled it. The checkout page will show the payment method currently available.

How are delivery charges calculated?

Delivery charges depend on the store settings and are shown during checkout. The store administrator must configure the correct delivery amount and service area before accepting live orders.

Can I request a bulk order?

Yes. Use the Get Quote link and submit your product requirements, quantity and contact details. The store team can review the enquiry and respond with the next steps.

How can I track my order?

Sign in using the email address associated with your order and open the My Orders page. Order status information is shown there when available.

Do I need an account to shop?

You can browse the catalogue without signing in. Create an account or sign in when required by checkout, and use the same email address to view your order history.

How can I contact JRT Mart?

Use the Contact page to find the support details entered by the JRT Mart administrator. Add verified phone, email and business hours before publishing.

Are the prices shown final?

Product prices are maintained by the store administrator. Applicable taxes, delivery charges and the final payable amount should be reviewed on the checkout screen before payment.

Can I cancel or return an order?

Cancellation, return, exchange and refund conditions must follow the published JRT Mart policies. The administrator must add accurate policy information before accepting orders.

When will my order be dispatched?

Dispatch estimates depend on the product and fulfilment settings. Review any delivery estimate shown on the product or checkout page, or contact the store for clarification.

Are all products always available?

Only products published by the store appear in the active catalogue. Stock and availability can change, so confirm the current status on the product page before ordering.

Does JRT Mart manufacture every product?

JRT Mart is presented here as an online store. Product brands, sellers, origin and manufacturing details should be stated on the relevant product listing where verified; this website does not claim that JRT Mart owns factories or manufactures every listed item.
